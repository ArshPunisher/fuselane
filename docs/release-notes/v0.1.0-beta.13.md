The browser extension now takes every download, like IDM, with a live popup, and hand-offs to another computer start there at once. Beta.12 offers this update by itself: click **Update and restart**.

## New

- **Every download from the browser**: with the extension, downloads go to Fuselane as you click them. The browser never starts its own copy, and the window stays in the background; it only comes forward when there's something to choose, such as a video's quality.
- **A new extension popup**: total speed with each network's share drawn as a lane, each download as a tile with its kind of file, percentage and speed, finished downloads under **Done**, and the page's video one tap away. The popup opens on Downloads when a download starts.
- **Without the app**: the extension downloads small files by itself, and for big ones offers the app or the browser.
- **Hand-offs start on the other computer at once**: it takes what's already downloaded from this computer and fetches the rest from the internet at the same time. While the other computer hasn't accepted, a loader says so; once it accepts, you land on the Send page and see it going.
- **Same link again**: clicking a link that's already in Fuselane, paused or stopped, asks whether to resume it or download a new copy.
- **Cancelling asks first**, in the app and in the extension.
- **Video pages are read as soon as the popup opens**, and HD video comes as one item with its sound.

## Fixed

- **Pause is instant**: it used to wait for a stuck connection, the server's first answer or the checksum lookup, which could take 5 to 10 seconds. It now stops at once at any point.
- **Downloads start sooner**: the checksum next to a file is looked up in every place at once (about 5 s faster on some servers).
- **Progress counts what was already downloaded** after a resume, instead of starting from 0%.
- **The app and the extension show a download as running the moment it runs**, with bytes as they arrive.
- **A download from the browser is selected in the app**, instead of the old selection staying put.
- **Computers you trust show up in Send** without switching to Everyone first.
- **Sending progress updates smoothly** instead of jumping from 0 to halfway.
- **Links in open pages keep working** after the extension is reloaded or updated.

## macOS: asked once more, then never again

Fuselane is now signed with its own certificate. macOS asks one more time for access to the Downloads folder after this update, and then remembers it through future updates.

## Browser extension 0.4.0

The extension has its own update, through the browser stores: the new popup, every download to Fuselane, downloading without the app, the "already in Fuselane" question and cancel that asks first.
