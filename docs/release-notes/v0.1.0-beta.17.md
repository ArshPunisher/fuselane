Phone sends no longer flood the list, and a video page opened from the extension goes straight to the video. Beta.15 offers this update by itself: click **Update and restart**.

## New

- **Send many files as one tile**: when a phone or another computer sends a whole selection, the Send page shows one tile ("23 of 451 files") with one progress bar instead of one tile per file. Files sent one by one keep their own tile. Cancel on the tile stops the files that haven't started yet too.
- **Videos open straight in Get the video**: when the extension's **Open this page in Fuselane** button is used on a single YouTube or Vimeo video page, the app goes to the loader and then the quality list, with no extra click.

## Fixed

- **Send order**: a new send now shows at the top of the list instead of landing between older transfers.
- **Security**: updated the TLS library (rustls 0.23.46), which fixes four advisories (RUSTSEC-2026-0346 to 0349), and moved the zip reader to zip 9.0.4 after its maintainers withdrew every older release. Fuselane never used the affected extract call, and it still refuses absolute and escaping paths inside zip files.

Please report problems with **Settings → Report a problem**, or at https://github.com/ArshPunisher/fuselane/issues.
