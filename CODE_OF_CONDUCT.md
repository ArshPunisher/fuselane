# Code of conduct

Fuselane follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

Be kind and patient. Welcome newcomers, including people who are new to open source or who don't write English well. Disagree about ideas, not about people.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, and anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome.

This applies in the issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code to the maintainer, [@ArshPunisher](https://github.com/ArshPunisher), through a [private security report](https://github.com/ArshPunisher/fuselane/security/advisories/new) (it reaches only the maintainer) or a direct message on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
