First time opening Fuselane from the disk image?

Fuselane is open source and signed on your Mac, but not notarized by Apple (that needs a paid account). The first time, macOS stops it once:

  1. Drag Fuselane into Applications and open it. macOS says it can't check it.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to Security and choose Open Anyway, then Open.

After that it opens like any other app. The command above skips this step.

Windows says it protected your PC?

Code signing through the SignPath Foundation is being set up. Until then, SmartScreen may stop a new app it hasn't seen often:

  1. Choose More info.
  2. Choose Run anyway.

Fuselane installs for your account only and updates itself after that.
