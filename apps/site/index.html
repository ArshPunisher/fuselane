New: Fuse Send

Send a file of any size. Nothing to upload.

Pick a file, copy the link, send it however you like. The receiver gets it straight from your computer, encrypted on the way and checked when it lands. No cloud in the middle, so no size limit, no expiry, no account.

  • End-to-end encrypted: the key lives only in the link
  • No size limit: 4 GB or 400 GB, same link
  • Same Wi-Fi: straight away. Across the internet: over IPv6, or when the sender's router has UPnP on
  • Survives a restart: the same link keeps working

Built in, so you need fewer apps

Things that usually take a separate app, or a subscription. Free, and on your computer only.

  • Videos from pagesYouTube and 1,000+ sites, in the quality you pick, over every network.
  • Network checkSpeed, delay and lag under load per network, plus a report for your provider.
  • Do this nowOne download gets every network; the rest wait their turn.
  • Ready bySet a time it should be done. Fuselane says if it's on track.
  • Groups and whole pagesGrab every file on a downloads page as one group.
  • Verified by itselfPublished checksums are found and checked for you.
  • Text between computersCopy on one, paste on the other, phones too. Over your own network.
  • Folders in syncKeep a folder mirrored on your other computer. No cloud.
  • FeedsNew podcast episodes and releases download by themselves.
  • Data usedWhat each network carried, day by day, for two months.
  • Hours per networkUse the phone only on its night plan; downloads move over.
  • Works with aria2 appsAriaNg and the Aria2 extensions can add downloads here.
